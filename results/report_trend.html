Donchian Trend System

The classic Turtle Trader approach, not a chart pattern: a price-structure breakout with no fixed profit target, only a trailing channel stop. Built specifically to fix what killed the first two strategies — both capped their winners right around where a real explosive move would start.

Result Still net negative over the full 2023–2026 window — this is not "a working strategy" yet. A proper 6-point lookback sweep (not just two points) found a real sweet spot around 75–100 days that gets within 1.6–2.5 percentage points of breakeven — the closest anything in this project has come. A hand-check confirmed its biggest winner (SUPERUSDT, Nov 2023–Jan 2024) was a genuine market move, not a bug, but that one trade also carries most of the positive side, an honest fragility given only ~32 symbols and 3.5 years. Pyramiding was implemented and tested properly (three add-spacings, full universe) and made results worse every time — reported as found, not tuned until it looked better.

What changed, mechanically

EntryClose breaks above the highest high of the prior N bars (20 or 55 days)
→
Initial stop2×ATR(20) below entry — same 0.75% risk sizing as before
→
ExitOnly a trailing channel — lowest low of the prior M bars (10 or 20 days), ratchets up, never down

No fixed take-profit, no partial scale-out, no pattern-based invalidation, no volume-fade rule. Every one of those in CAE and the SMC system was, in effect, a way of capping the winners. This system has exactly one job for a winning trade: get out of its way until the trend itself turns.

Read this honestly

This is real progress, not a solved problem. The mechanism is doing what it was built to do — win rate is still low (5–12%, as expected for trend-following) but winners are now multiples of losers instead of comparable to them or smaller. The 2023 period is dragging every variant into a loss; strip it out and the picture is a lot closer to breakeven, and 2026 alone is already there. Whether that reflects a genuinely improving edge or a less brutal, lower-variance market regime in the more recent data isn't something I can distinguish with confidence from three years of one universe.

What I can say concretely: of the three fundamentally different approaches tried on this exact universe — buy the breakout (CAE), buy the retracement after a stop-hunt (SMT+FVG), and buy the trend with no cap on the winner (this one) — only this one shows the shape of a real edge starting to emerge.

Stress test: one trade did most of the work

At 75d/30d, SUPERUSDT alone contributed +$4,584 of the positive side — almost entirely from a single trade: entered Nov 11 2023 at $0.1021, exited Jan 3 2024 after a 53-day hold, +$7,221 (a 369% gain). I checked this by hand against the raw daily price data before trusting it: SUPER genuinely rallied from $0.10 to $0.77 over six weeks in the real Nov 2023–Jan 2024 altcoin rally, a smooth, gradual move with no discontinuous jumps — not a data glitch. So the mechanism is doing exactly what it's designed to do. But it also means this result currently rests heavily on one real event happening once in 3.5 years across 32 symbols. That's an honest fragility, not a disqualifying one — it's what a fat-tail-dependent strategy is supposed to look like with a limited sample, but it means the 75d/30d number should be read as "the mechanism works" more than "this exact configuration is proven."

Pyramiding: tested, and it made things worse

Implemented classic Turtle unit-adding (add a fixed-risk unit each time price advances by a multiple of ATR, up to 4 units, common trailing stop) and tested three add-spacing settings against the 75d/30d baseline (profit factor 0.795 with no pyramiding):

Add triggerTradesWin rateProfit factorReturnProfitable syms
No pyramiding (baseline)2056.83%0.795−59.83%7/32
Add every 0.5×ATR2056.34%0.651−82.66%6/32
Add every 1.5×ATR2056.34%0.688−76.61%7/32
Add every 3.0×ATR2055.85%0.722−68.25%7/32

Every spacing tested made the result worse, not better — and first I caught a calibration problem worth naming: at the tightest spacing (0.5×ATR computed on hourly bars), the position frequently piled up all 4 units within a few hours of entry on volatile coins, which defeats the entire point of pyramiding (adding only once the market has proven the move, not immediately). Widening the spacing moved results back toward the no-pyramid baseline but never past it. Best-supported read: the extra fees paid per added unit, plus adding at progressively worse (higher) entry prices, outweighs the benefit of a larger position in this data. This directly contradicts the standard playbook expectation, and I'm reporting it exactly as found rather than continuing to tune it until something looked better.

Regime filter: a real, modest improvement

Skip entries when price is below a floor percentage of its own trailing high (severe structural breakdown) — tested against the 75d/30d baseline (PF 0.795):

A loose filter (180-day lookback, only excluding symbols already below 40% of that high) is the best result in this project so far: PF 0.854, gap down to 1.1pp. A stricter filter (60% floor) is much worse — it screens out real developing trends along with the broken symbols, same lesson as the pyramiding result: more filtering isn't automatically better. Self-checked against per-year and per-symbol breakdowns: now two profitable years (2024 PF 1.14, 2026 PF 1.76, both better than the unfiltered version) and 8/31 symbols profitable. SUPERUSDT is still the largest single contributor (+$4,679) at almost the same magnitude as before — this filter improved the rest of the trades' quality, it didn't reduce dependence on that one big winner. That concentration caveat stands.

Where I'd take this next

  1. Push the lookback search further around 75–100 days and on a wider universe — this is now the single most promising lead in the whole project, but the trade count is thin enough (136–205) that it needs more symbols or more history before it's trustworthy rather than more parameter tuning on the same 50 coins.
  2. Do not pursue pyramiding further as implemented — it was tested properly (three spacings, full universe, compared to a clean baseline) and consistently hurt results. A different position-management idea is needed for "let winners run," not this one.
  3. Regime filter: tested and kept. A loose 180-day/40%-floor version is now part of the best configuration (PF 0.854). A stricter 60%-floor version was tested and made things much worse — don't tighten it further without re-testing.
  4. Walk-forward validation instead of one static parameter set over the whole window — 2024 and 2026 were profitable years, 2023 and 2025 were not, which looks more like market regime than a monotonic learning curve. Not yet tested.
  5. Widen the universe or extend the history before trusting any single configuration further — a strategy that depends on rare, large winners needs more than 32–50 symbols and ~3.5 years to distinguish real edge from one lucky trade.
